GOOD TO KNOW

A Few Things You Might Be Wondering.

Have questions before booking? Here are a few details to help you plan your culinary experience with confidence.

What does a private chef service include?

Depending on your booking, the service may include menu planning, ingredient sourcing, on-site cooking, plating, and agreed kitchen cleanup. All inclusions will be confirmed before your event.

Can I customize the menu?

Absolutely. Menus can be tailored to your taste, occasion, dietary preferences, and budget. We will discuss your requirements before confirming your booking.

How far in advance should I book?

Advance booking is recommended, particularly for weekends, holidays, and special celebrations. Your date is confirmed once availability and booking arrangements have been agreed.

Do you cater for large events?

Event capacity depends on the menu, location, kitchen facilities, and staffing requirements. Share your guest count so we can discuss a suitable arrangement.

Can you prepare Nigerian dishes?

Nigerian-inspired cuisine can be included in your experience, depending on the chef's specialties. Options may include jollof rice, grilled proteins, traditional soups, and modern Nigerian fusion dishes.

How do I confirm my booking?

Submit the booking enquiry form with your preferred date and event details. We can then discuss availability, pricing, payment arrangements, and the next steps for confirmation.
